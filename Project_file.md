# Dashboard Development – Overview Page

## 1. Page Overview

The Overview page provides a consolidated view of the supply chain and logistics operations of Samsung.

It presents high-level performance indicators and provides navigation into four major analytical areas:

- Supplier
- Inventory
- Shipment
- Customer

The objective is to provide a quick understanding of overall business performance while allowing users to explore individual operational areas.

## 3. Analytical Sections

### Supplier Analysis
- Supplier initiations
- Lead time comparison between suppliers
- Identification of suppliers with lower lead times

### Inventory Analysis
- Manufacturer initiations
- Inventory value
- Product-wise inventory comparison

### Shipment Analysis
- Shipment initiations
- Shipment quantity
- Carrier-wise shipment delay comparison

### Customer Analysis
- Customer initiations
- Delivered quantity
- Revenue comparison across customer sales platforms

## 4. Visualizations Used

| Visualization | Analytical Purpose |
|---|---|
| KPI Cards | Display key business metrics |
| Donut Charts | Display proportional comparisons |
| Horizontal Bar Charts | Compare suppliers, products and carriers |
| Slicers | Allow interactive filtering |
| Navigation Cards | Provide access to different analytical sections |

## 5. Dashboard Design

- Samsung-inspired visual identity.
- Blue and lavender background palette.
- Consistent KPI card styling.
- Section-based navigation.
- Combination of summary metrics and detailed comparisons.
